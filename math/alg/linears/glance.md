title: The same symbols, harder ideas
---

::: card
The notation scales well past straight lines. A few forms you will meet:

$$ \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1 \qquad \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6} $$
:::

::: card
$$ \int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3} \qquad \binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!\,(n-k)!} $$
:::

::: card
$$ |x| = \begin{cases} x & x \ge 0 \\ -x & x < 0 \end{cases} $$
:::
