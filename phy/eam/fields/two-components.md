---
title: Two components, one principle
---

::: card
Resistors convert energy to heat immediately. Capacitors and inductors store it in fields and
give it back. That single difference, storage versus dissipation, determines how they behave in
a circuit.
:::

::: card
A capacitor stores energy in an electric field between its plates and resists a change in
voltage. An inductor stores energy in a magnetic field inside its coil and resists a change in
current.
:::

::: card
Filling and emptying a field takes time, and time is how these components shape signals, filter
frequencies, and give circuits memory. The electric and magnetic fields here are two aspects of
one thing, the electromagnetic field; how they generate each other, and produce light, comes in
a later part.
:::

::: exercise q1
A capacitor resists a change in voltage. What does an inductor resist a change in?

::: answer
Current. An inductor opposes a change in the current through it, storing energy in its magnetic
field.
:::
:::
