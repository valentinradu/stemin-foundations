---
title: The shape of alternating current
---

::: card
Direct current flows one way at a steady level, the kind a battery delivers. Alternating current
does the opposite: it reverses direction over and over, many times a second, its voltage tracing
a smooth sine. It rises to a peak, falls back through zero to an equal negative peak, and
returns. One full there-and-back is a **cycle**.
:::

::: card
The standard rate is 50 cycles per second, 50 **hertz**, across most of the world; the Americas
use 60 Hz. Nothing physical prefers one number over the other; each grid fixed a rate early so
that every appliance and meter built for it would interoperate.
:::

::: exercise q1
European mains runs at 50 Hz. How long is one cycle?

::: answer
20 ms. The period is the reciprocal of the frequency, $T = 1/50 = 0.02$ s.
:::
:::
