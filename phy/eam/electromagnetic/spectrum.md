---
title: The spectrum
---

::: card
A charge oscillating at any frequency produces a wave at that frequency. All frequencies are the
same phenomenon, differing only in how fast the charge oscillates.
:::

::: card
From low to high: radio (kHz to GHz), microwave (GHz), infrared (THz), visible light (430 to
770 THz, 700 to 400 nm), ultraviolet, X-ray, and gamma. One field, one mechanism, the entire
spectrum. The only difference is frequency.
:::

::: exercise q1
A radio antenna broadcasts at 100 MHz but also radiates weakly at 200 MHz and 300 MHz. What are
these, and why do they occur?

::: answer
Harmonics: integer multiples of the fundamental. Non-linear components in the transmitter distort
the sine wave, generating them. They are weaker than the fundamental but real, and must be
filtered to avoid interfering with other bands.
:::
:::
